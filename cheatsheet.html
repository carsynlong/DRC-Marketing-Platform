GAME NIGHT PLAN

Before Friday

  • On the laptop: open whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/host, unlock Side quests with your PIN, click 🧪 Fill with test data, and click through the show with a couple of real phones.
  • Click 🧹 End rehearsal twice. That wipes test victors, points, phones and turns off chaos, sponsor gifts and the clock.
  • Print the bingo cards (2 per page, landscape, Background graphics on, margins None).

Game night, before everyone arrives

  • Plug the laptop into the TV. Turn up the laptop and TV volume (sound must go to the TV).
  • Open whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/check in Chrome. Fix anything red. Click Test for sound, voice and camera.
  • Open whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/show, click to start, press F for fullscreen. Leave the Lobby up.
  • On your phone: scan the QR code, tap your face, enter your PIN. Your remote and scoreboard are at the top.
  • Have ready: cookies, blindfolds, spoons, bowls and cotton snowballs, 3 dice and chips, bingo cards and pens, the bingo cage, the major awards.

Keys on the laptop

Games, the wheel and bingo open in a new tab. Close that tab to come back to the same slide.

⚙ Menu (top left of the show)

  • Go to the beginning / Skip to the end
  • Jump to The Capitol Has Spoken
  • Clear phone sign-ins
  • Photo booth, recap, awards, draft order, birthday, bounty board, crown
  • Chaos mode and ticker on/off

On every game slide (top right buttons)

  • 🏆 Record victor: tap the winner. Then 🥈 Battle for Silver: tap everyone who was close, FIGHT, tap who won. Only one person close? Tap them, Award 2nd place.
  • 🎁 Unbox (after a victor): they open the major award, you rate it Treasure, Mystery or Junk. 🎤 Interview: Caesar-style questions.
  • ⚔️ Tiebreaker (physical and luck games): tap who tied, pick a tiebreaker, tap who won.
  • Bets on that game pay out on their own when you record the victor.

☕ Intermission (I)

Break with an optional 5, 10 or 15 minute timer. ▶ Resume is in the top right (or Esc).

🎥 Live camera (W)

The room on the TV as "LIVE FROM THE ARENA". Chrome asks for the camera the first time.

TAB BY TAB

📋 Scoreboard (tap to open, top of your phone)

Every game's 🥇 and 🥈, bingo rounds, tiebreakers, Capitol Points and the Ultimate Victor. Below it: 🎬 your coach (what to do on the slide that's on the TV) and red 🆘 help alerts when a guest taps "I'm lost" (tap On my way).

🎮 Show TAB

  • The show: Back, Next, Close pop-up, Intermission, Live cam, Mute.
  • ⏰ Arena clock 5 to 30 min (alarm at 0) · 🎲 Chaos ON/OFF (random twist every 4 to 8 min) · 📺 Ticker ON/OFF · 📼 Night recap.
  • 🔊 Your voice: type it, the announcer says it on the TV.

⚔️ Quests TAB

  • Side quest: pick a quest (or write one), 10/15/30/60 seconds, Send. TV and their phone count down. Then ✅ They did it (+1) or ❌ Failed (-1).
  • 🎲 Double or nothing: turn on before Send. Done doubles their points, failed loses that much.
  • ⭐ +1 / 💀 -1 to the picked tribute · 📊 Points on TV.

🌀 Twists TAB

  • On the TV: 🎰 Wheel of Punishment · 🔁 Body swap · 🤐 Silence · 👑 QUEEN MOTHER decree · 🎯 Bounty (always the points leader) · 🐦 Alliance · 🕵️ Secret mission (only their phone sees it).
  • Quick twists: 🔥 Fireballs · ⚡ Lightning (swap seats) · 🐺 Mutts · ❄️ Freeze · 🎁 Bonus major award · 📜 New rule · 🍗 The Feast · 🪂 Sponsor gift · 🧽 Clear banners.
  • 🐺 Sabotage the online games: ❄️ Freeze · 🐕 Mutt bite · 🌫️ Fog · 🙃 Flip · 🐝 Tracker jackers · 🧊 Freeze everyone BUT picked.

📱 Phones TAB

  • 🗳️ Vote: pick a question, Open, then Reveal the winner. 💰 Bets: pick the game, Open, Lock when it starts. Pays out when you record that game's victor.
  • 📲 Tap race to 30, 50 or 100 (+1 point) · 🎭 Hot seat (tap a question to put it on the TV) · 🏳️ Mercy vote then ⚖️ Verdict.
  • 🔮 Predictions: Open early, Lock, Reveal at the end (+3 if right). Shout-outs from phones show up here: ✅ to put one on the TV.

🎉 Fun TAB

  • 🎙️ Soundboard: air horn, cheer, applause, Ohhh!, laugh, rimshot, sad trombone, cannon, drumroll, siren, fanfare, boom.
  • 🎂 QUEEN MOTHER's birthday · 📜 Bounty board · 👑 Crown the Ultimate Victor · 📸 Photo booth · 🎁 Award draft order · 🏁 End-of-night awards · 📱 Phone tour on TV (play it in the Lobby) · ⭐ Explain Capitol Points (also pops up on its own the first time points happen).

🧯 If something goes wrong

  • A pop-up won't close or the arrows stop working: press Esc.
  • No sound: check the laptop and TV volume and that sound goes to the TV. Press M in case it's muted.
  • The TV looks old or wrong: press Cmd+Shift+R to reload. You land on the same slide.
  • A phone looks wrong: reload the page. Name taken by someone else? Tap "Not you?" and pick again.
  • Phones stuck or someone's on the wrong name: ⚙ menu, Clear phone sign-ins. Everyone scans again.
  • No internet: the show keeps going. Phone extras pause and come back on their own.
  • Twists or points don't work from the laptop: unlock the PIN on /host (Side quests).

Suggested big moments

  • Lobby: 🔮 open predictions. Before each game: 💰 open bets, ⏰ start the arena clock.
  • During games: ⚔️ a side quest, a 🌀 twist or 🐺 sabotage. Halftime: 🎂 QUEEN MOTHER's birthday, 📲 tap race.
  • Before slide 43: 🏁 end-of-night awards, 📼 night recap, 🔮 reveal predictions, 👑 crown the Ultimate Victor, 🎁 award draft order.
