Gamemaker Cheat Sheet

The 2026 Christmas Games, Thanksmas Edition. Show link: whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/show

Before everyone arrives

  • Plug the laptop into the TV. Open the show link in Chrome and click to start. Press F for fullscreen.
  • Turn the volume up. Test sound on the lobby screen (lo-fi music should be playing).
  • Have ready: cookies, blindfolds, spoons, bowls and cotton snowballs, 3 dice and chips, bingo cards and pens, the bingo cage, major awards.
  • Clear test names: on The Capitol Has Spoken (slide 43), tap each game, delete the name, press Enter.

Phones

  • Everyone scans the QR code on the Lobby (or Intermission) screen, or goes to whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/showphone, and taps their face.
  • Their phone shows what's on the TV, and has emoji buttons that pop up on the TV with their name.
  • 🎥 Go live on the TV: their phone camera shows in a round "LIVE" bubble on the right side of the TV for 20 seconds.
  • A small QR code stays in the top right of every slide, so anyone who closes the page can scan back in.
  • Your phone remote: join as Carsyn and enter PIN 1185. You get side quests, points, Back, Next, Close pop-up, Intermission, Live cam, Mute and all the twists.

Gamemaker Menu and twists

  • Open whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/host on the laptop. It starts the show, jumps to any game, opens the wheel, bingo and games, and has Reset everything for game night.
  • Twists (only you): 🔥 Fireballs, ⚡ Lightning storm (swap seats), 🐺 Mutts (stand and spin, last one loses a turn), ❄️ Freeze (5-second countdown), 🎁 Bonus major award (next to make you laugh), 📜 New rule (stays on screen until you tap it or Clear the rule), 🍗 The Feast (snack break).

Side quests, sponsor gifts and Capitol Points

  • ⚔️ Side quest: on your phone remote or the host menu, tap a tribute (or 🎲 Random), pick a quest (or write your own), pick 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds, Send. The TV and their phone count down. Then tap ✅ They did it (+1) or ❌ Failed (-1).
  • 🪂 Sponsor gift: only tributes who haven't won a game yet. 3 of them face off in a quick challenge, and you tap the winner for +2 points. Drop one any time, or turn on Random sponsor gifts (one every 8 to 15 minutes).
  • 📊 Points on TV shows the standings. ⭐ +1 / 💀 -1 gives or takes a point from whoever you tapped. Reset points on the host menu before game night.

On every game slide (top right)

  • 🏆 Record victor: tap the winner's photo. Then comes 🥈 The Battle for Silver: tap everyone who was close, press FIGHT, and they face off in a random challenge (thumb war, staring contest and more). Tap who won. Only one person close? Tap them and Award 2nd place. 1st and 2nd each win a major award.
  • 🎁 Unbox: appears after a victor is recorded. They open the major award, you rate it Treasure, Mystery or Junk.
  • ⚔️ Tiebreaker: on the physical and luck games. Tap who tied, pick a tiebreaker, tap who won.
  • 🎤 Interview: appears after a victor is recorded. Caesar-style questions for the winner to answer out loud.
  • 🎥 Live broadcast (bottom left, or W): the room on the TV through the laptop camera, as "LIVE FROM THE ARENA". Chrome asks to allow the camera the first time.
  • ☕ Intermission (bottom left, or I): bathroom and drink break with an optional 5, 10 or 15 minute timer. Resume takes you back to the same slide.
