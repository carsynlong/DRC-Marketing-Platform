GAME NIGHT PLAN

Before Friday

  • On the laptop: open whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/host, unlock Side quests with your PIN, click 🧪 Fill with test data, and click through the show with a couple of real phones.
  • When you're done practicing: ⚙ menu on the TV, 🔄 Start completely over. Wipes winners, points, news, photos and phone sign-ins (your PIN stays).
  • Print the bingo cards (2 per page, landscape, Background graphics on, margins None).

Game night, before everyone arrives

  • Plug the laptop into the TV. Turn up the laptop and TV volume (sound must go to the TV).
  • Open whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/check in Chrome. Fix anything red. Click Test for sound, voice and camera.
  • Open whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/show, click to start, press F for fullscreen. Leave the Lobby up.
  • On your phone: scan the QR code, tap your face (Clo), enter your PIN. Your remote is on your phone, with Back, Close, Start, Next and Mute stuck to the bottom.
  • Have ready: cookies, blindfolds, spoons, bowls and cotton snowballs, 3 dice and chips, bingo cards and pens, the bingo cage, the major awards.

⚙ Menu (top left of the show)

  • Go to the beginning / Skip to the end / The Capitol Has Spoken
  • Photo booth, recap, 🕯️ Fallen Tributes, awards, draft, birthday, crown, 🏆 certificates, 🎟️ 2027 Reaping
  • 🧹 Clear winners and award stats
  • 🔄 Start completely over

On every game slide (top right buttons)

  • 🏆 Record victor: tap the winner. Then 🥈 Battle for Silver: tap everyone who was close, FIGHT, tap who won. Only one person close? Tap them, Award 2nd place.
  • 🎁 Unbox (after a victor): they open the major award, you rate it Treasure, Mystery or Junk. 🎤 Interview: Caesar-style questions.
  • ⚔️ Tiebreaker (physical and luck games): tap who tied, pick a tiebreaker, tap who won.
  • Bets on that game pay out on their own when you record the victor.

☕ Intermission (I)

Break with an optional 5, 10 or 15 minute timer. ▶ Resume is in the top right (or Esc).

🎥 Live camera (W)

The room on the TV as "LIVE FROM THE ARENA". Chrome asks for the camera the first time.

TAB BY TAB

📌 Always on your phone

  • Bottom bar: ⬅️ Back · ✖️ Close pop-up · ▶ Start (rules screens) · ➡️ Next · 🔇 Mute.
  • 👆 On the TV right now: every button on the TV shows here. Tap it and the TV presses it (record victor, 2nd place, tiebreakers, wheel SPIN, bingo).
  • 🎬 Coach says what to do on this slide · 📋 Scoreboard (tap to open) · red 🆘 help alerts (tap On my way).
  • Big buttons need two taps: the first turns red.

🎮 Show TAB

  • 📍 Jump to a slide · 🔊 Announce (type it, the announcer says it) · quick sounds: air horn, applause, drumroll.
  • Breaks: ☕ Intermission · 🎥 Live cam · ⏰ 10 or 20 min clock · ⏹️ Stop clock.

⚔️ Quests TAB

  • 👤 Picked: … tap to choose who. Pick a quest, the seconds, 🎲 Double if you want, then Send.
  • Then ✅ Did it (+1) or ❌ Failed (-1). Also ⭐ +1 / 💀 -1 and 📊 Points on TV.

🌀 Twists TAB

  • 🎯 On the picked tribute: Wheel of Punishment, body swap, silence, decree, bounty, alliance, secret mission.
  • 🌍 Whole room: fireballs, lightning, mutts, freeze, bonus major award, new rule, the Feast, clear banners.

📱 Phones TAB

  • Shout-outs and hot seat questions to approve sit at the top (a number on the tab tells you).
  • 🗳️ Vote: Open, then Reveal. 💰 Bets: Open, Lock, Pay out. 📲 Tap race: 30, 50 or 100.
  • 🎭 Hot seat · 🏳️ Mercy vote · 🔮 Predictions (Open early, Reveal at the end). Buttons only show when they apply.

🎉 Fun TAB

  • 🎙️ Soundboard · Big moments: birthday, photo booth, bounty board, night recap, 🕯️ Fallen Tributes, crown, draft order, end-of-night awards.
  • ⚙ Settings (folded): News ticker ON or OFF. Nothing pops up on its own.
  • 🛠️ Setup and reset (folded): phone tour, explain points, sign out all phones, clear winners and award stats, start completely over.

🧯 If something goes wrong

  • A pop-up won't close or the arrows stop working: press Esc.
  • No sound: check the laptop and TV volume and that sound goes to the TV. Press M in case it's muted.
  • The TV looks old or wrong: press Cmd+Shift+R to reload. You land on the same slide.
  • A phone looks wrong: reload the page. Name taken by someone else? Tap "Not you?" and pick again.
  • Phones stuck or someone's on the wrong name: Fun tab, 🛠️ Setup and reset, Sign out all phones. Everyone scans again.
  • No internet: the show keeps going. Phone extras pause and come back on their own.
  • Twists or points don't work from the laptop: unlock the PIN on /host (Side quests).

Suggested big moments

  • Lobby: 🔮 open predictions. Before each game: 💰 open bets, ⏰ start the arena clock.
  • During games: ⚔️ a side quest, or a 🌀 twist. Halftime: 🎂 QUEEN MOTHER's birthday, 📲 tap race.
  • Before slide 44: 🏁 end-of-night awards, 📼 night recap, 🔮 reveal predictions, 👑 crown the Ultimate Victor, 🎁 award draft order. At the end: 🕯️ Fallen Tributes.
