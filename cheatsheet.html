Gamemaker Cheat Sheet

The 2026 Christmas Games, Thanksmas Edition. Show link: whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/show

Before everyone arrives

  • 🩺 Run the Game Night Check first: whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/check on the TV laptop. Fix anything red, test sound, voice and camera.
  • Plug the laptop into the TV. Open the show link in Chrome and click to start. Press F for fullscreen.
  • Turn the volume up. Test sound on the lobby screen (lo-fi music should be playing).
  • Have ready: cookies, blindfolds, spoons, bowls and cotton snowballs, 3 dice and chips, bingo cards and pens, the bingo cage, major awards.
  • Clear test names: on The Capitol Has Spoken (slide 43), tap each game, delete the name, press Enter.

⚙ Host menu on the show

  • Faint ⚙ in the top left corner of the show (hover to see it). Go to the beginning, Skip to the end, Jump to The Capitol Has Spoken, Clear phone sign-ins (everyone scans and taps their face again), Open the Gamemaker menu.

Phones

  • Everyone scans the QR code on the Lobby (or Intermission) screen, or goes to whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/showphone, and taps their face.
  • Their phone shows what's on the TV, and has emoji buttons that pop up on the TV with their name.
  • 🎥 Go live on the TV: their phone camera shows in a round "LIVE" bubble on the right side of the TV for 20 seconds.
  • A small QR code stays in the top right of every slide, so anyone who closes the page can scan back in.
  • Your phone remote: join as Carsyn and enter your PIN (keep it secret). You get side quests, points, Back, Next, Close pop-up, Intermission, Live cam, Mute and all the twists.

Gamemaker Menu and twists

  • Open whos-that-tribute.vercel.app/host on the laptop. It starts the show, jumps to any game, opens the wheel, bingo and games, and has Reset everything for game night.
  • Twists (only you): 🔥 Fireballs, ⚡ Lightning storm (swap seats), 🐺 Mutts (stand and spin, last one loses a turn), ❄️ Freeze (5-second countdown), 🎁 Bonus major award (next to make you laugh), 📜 New rule (stays on screen until you tap it or Clear the rule), 🍗 The Feast (snack break).

Side quests, sponsor gifts and Capitol Points

  • ⚔️ Side quest: on your phone remote or the host menu, tap a tribute (or 🎲 Random), pick a quest (or write your own), pick 10, 15, 30 or 60 seconds, Send. The TV and their phone count down. Then tap ✅ They did it (+1) or ❌ Failed (-1).
  • 🪂 Sponsor gift: only tributes who haven't won a game yet. 3 of them face off in a quick challenge, and you tap the winner for +2 points. Drop one any time, or turn on Random sponsor gifts (one every 8 to 15 minutes).
  • 🐺 Sabotage (during Who's That Tribute, District Feud and District Secrets): tap a tribute (or 🎲 Random), then ❄️ Freeze (no taps for 8 seconds), 🐕 Mutt bite (25 seconds where every tap makes them limp), 🌫️ Fog, 🙃 Flip, or 🐝 Tracker jackers. It hits their phone and the game's TV shows who got sabotaged.
  • 🌀 More twists (tap a tribute first, or none for random): 🎰 Wheel of Punishment, 🔁 Body swap, 🤐 Silence, 👑 QUEEN MOTHER's decree (type your own on the host menu, or random), 🎯 Bounty on the points leader, 🐦 Alliance, 🕵️ Secret mission (only their phone sees it; your remote shows you what it was). Reminder banners stay at the top of the TV: tap one, or Clear banners.
  • 🎲 Double or nothing: turn it on before sending a side quest. Done doubles their points (at least +2). Failed loses that much.
  • 🧊 Freeze everyone BUT picked: in the online games, everyone except that tribute is frozen for 8 seconds.
  • 📊 Points on TV shows the standings. ⭐ +1 / 💀 -1 gives or takes a point from whoever you tapped. Reset points on the host menu before game night.

Votes, bets and more (your phone remote or the host menu)

  • 📋 Scoreboard at the top of your phone remote: every game's 🥇 and 🥈, bingo rounds, tiebreakers, Capitol Points, Ultimate Victor. Updates on its own from the TV laptop.
  • 🗳️ Phone vote: pick a question (or write one), Open the vote. Everyone taps a face on their phone, the TV counts live. Reveal the winner when ready.
  • 💰 Betting: pick the game, Open bets. People bet 1 to 3 points on who wins. Lock bets when the game starts. When you record the victor on the show, it asks to pay out (or tap the winner at the top of the remote, then Pay out).
  • 🎙️ Soundboard: air horn, Ohhh!, rimshot, sad trombone, cannon and more play on the TV.
  • 🚨 Snitch: anyone can report someone from their phone (fell asleep, cheating...). It pops up on the TV. You decide the punishment.
  • 🎵 Walk-up songs: everyone picks one on their phone. It plays on the TV a few seconds after you record them as a victor.
  • 📸 Photo booth (only when you tap it): framed photo from the laptop camera. They play as a slideshow on the last slide.
  • 🎁 Major award draft order: most Capitol Points picks first. 🏁 End-of-night awards: Most Sabotaged, Biggest Snitch, Loudest Fan, Camera Hog and more. Both are also in the ⚙ menu on the show.

Even more (remote, host menu, ⚙ menu)

  • 🧪 Rehearsal mode (host menu): fills in fake victors and points so you can click through. Purple badge on the TV. End rehearsal wipes it all.
  • 🔊 Your voice: type it, the announcer says it on the TV. ⏰ Arena clock: 5 to 30 minute countdown in the top left, alarm at 0.
  • 🎲 Chaos mode: a random twist, sabotage or sponsor gift every 4 to 8 minutes on its own. 📺 News ticker: live headlines along the bottom.
  • 🎭 Hot seat: phones send anonymous questions, you tap which ones go on the TV. 🏳️ Mercy vote: they beg, the room votes 👍 or 👎.
  • 🔮 Predictions: open early, phones pick the Ultimate Victor, Reveal at the end (+3 points if right). 💌 Shout-outs and 🤝 point trades come from phones; you approve shout-outs.
  • 📼 Night recap: victors, points, funniest moments and photo booth pictures.

Last additions

  • 📲 Tap race (Phones tab): first to 30, 50 or 100 taps on their phone wins a point, live race track on the TV.
  • 🎂 QUEEN MOTHER's birthday (Fun tab): takeover with balloons and the Happy Birthday song. Every phone gets a cake to tap. Granny's phone says "Happy Birthday, Your Majesty!"

On every game slide (top right)

  • 🏆 Record victor: tap the winner's photo. Then comes 🥈 The Battle for Silver: tap everyone who was close, press FIGHT, and they face off in a random challenge (thumb war, staring contest and more). Tap who won. Only one person close? Tap them and Award 2nd place. 1st and 2nd each win a major award.
  • 🎁 Unbox: appears after a victor is recorded. They open the major award, you rate it Treasure, Mystery or Junk.
  • ⚔️ Tiebreaker: on the physical and luck games. Tap who tied, pick a tiebreaker, tap who won.
  • 🎤 Interview: appears after a victor is recorded. Caesar-style questions for the winner to answer out loud.
  • 🎥 Live broadcast (bottom left, or W): the room on the TV through the laptop camera, as "LIVE FROM THE ARENA". Chrome asks to allow the camera the first time.
  • ☕ Intermission (bottom left, or I): bathroom and drink break with an optional 5, 10 or 15 minute timer. Resume takes you back to the same slide.
